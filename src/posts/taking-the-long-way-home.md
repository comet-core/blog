---
title: Taking the long way home
description: An extra turn, an unfamiliar street, and the small pleasure of having nowhere to hurry.
date: 2026-08-19
category: Everyday
sample: true
---
I took a left where I usually take a right.

There wasn’t a reason. The day had been full of reasons, and I was tired of having one for everything.

The other street had a shop I’d never noticed, though it looked as if it had been there longer than I’ve been alive. Someone had put a chair outside. A plant in an old paint tin leaned toward the pavement.

I walked slowly enough to read the notices in a window. A room to let. A missing cat, thankfully found: someone had written the good news across the bottom in blue pen.

By the time I got home, nothing had changed. I had no useful discovery to report. The walk had taken twelve extra minutes.

I think I got to keep all twelve.
