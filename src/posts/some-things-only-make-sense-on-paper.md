In my head, a thought can be everything at once. Urgent and ridiculous. Obvious and impossible to explain. It walks around wearing different coats.

On paper, it has to choose a sentence.

This is often where the trouble begins. I can spend twenty minutes trying to name a feeling that seemed perfectly clear before I picked up a pen. Sometimes all I manage is: *something about the way the evening looked.*

But that is a beginning. It’s a small door in a wall that was, a moment ago, only a wall.

## Permission to be unfinished

I’m trying to leave more first attempts alone. To resist going back over every sentence until it sounds like a person I don’t know, making a point I didn’t mean to make.

The notebook doesn’t need a conclusion. It can hold a question. It can hold half of one.

There’s something kind about that.

So here is today’s thought, without its coat: I don’t always write because I know what I want to say. Sometimes I write to find out what’s been keeping me company.
