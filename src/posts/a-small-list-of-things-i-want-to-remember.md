title: A small list of things I want to remember
description: Overheard words, the last light on a wall, and other things that almost got away.
date: 2026-08-24
category: Fragments
sample: true
---
- The particular click of a door closing softly, when someone is trying not to wake you.
- A stranger saying “take your time” and seeming to mean it.
- How the last light of the day can make an unremarkable wall look briefly necessary.
- The sentence I thought of on a walk and lost before I got home. I remember liking it. Maybe that’s enough.
- An old receipt tucked into a book, holding the date of a day I can no longer picture.
- The first few seconds after rain stops, before anything has decided what to do next.

Nothing to tie these together. Just a few things placed on the same page, so I can find them again.
